The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 61 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{1,2,5\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (4 subsets give this class), of order $432$; the smallest realization has degree 120 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 9$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 48, 484 and 1339; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
