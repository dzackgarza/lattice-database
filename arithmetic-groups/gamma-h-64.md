The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 64 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{0,1,2,3,7\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (4 subsets give this class), of order $384$; the smallest realization has degree 138 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 9$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 63, 653 and 1825; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
