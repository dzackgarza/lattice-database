The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 68 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{0,1,2,5\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (30 subsets give this class), of order $144$; the smallest realization has degree 160 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 10$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 72, 929 and 2637; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
