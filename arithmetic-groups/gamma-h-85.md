The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 85 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \{4,6\}$ of the Coxeter diagram (36 subsets give this class), of order $4$; the smallest realization has degree 580 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 20$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 292, 9246 and 27411; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
