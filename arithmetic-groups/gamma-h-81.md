The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 81 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \{4,6,5\}$ of the Coxeter diagram (9 subsets give this class), of order $24$; the smallest realization has degree 342 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 15$.
