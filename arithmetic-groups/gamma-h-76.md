The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 76 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \{4,6,8,5\}$ of the Coxeter diagram (1 subset gives this class), of order $192$; the smallest realization has degree 234 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 12$.
