The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 80 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \{4,6,-1,2\}$ of the Coxeter diagram (39 subsets give this class), of order $16$; the smallest realization has degree 340 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 15$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 163, 3626 and 10599; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
