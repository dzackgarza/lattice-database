The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 86 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \{4\}$ of the Coxeter diagram (10 subsets give this class), of order $2$; the smallest realization has degree 820 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 24$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 392, 14926 and 44387; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
