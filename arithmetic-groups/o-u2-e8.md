The full isometry group of $U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$, a subgroup of $O(L_{\mathbb R}) \cong O(1, 9)$; it is countably infinite, and its order, generators and orbits are stored in `automorphisms` on the card of [0125](../tag/0125.html).
Sterk writes it $O(M(2))$ for $M = H \oplus E_8(-1)$, whose twist $M(2) = U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ is this lattice (H. Sterk, *Compactifications of the period space of Enriques surfaces, Part I*, Math. Z. 207 (1991), 1–36, (4.3.5), p. 19).
