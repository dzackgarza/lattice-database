The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 72 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \{4,6,3,5\}$ of the Coxeter diagram (8 subsets give this class), of order $120$; the smallest realization has degree 198 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 11$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 92, 1210 and 3503; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
