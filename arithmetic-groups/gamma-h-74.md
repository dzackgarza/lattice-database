The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 74 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{0,1,2,5,7\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (64 subsets give this class), of order $48$; the smallest realization has degree 220 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 12$.
