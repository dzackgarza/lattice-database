The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 82 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \{4,6,3\}$ of the Coxeter diagram (54 subsets give this class), of order $12$; the smallest realization has degree 380 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 16$.
