The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 63 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{0,1,2,7,8\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (32 subsets give this class), of order $240$; the smallest realization has degree 132 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 9$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 70, 782 and 2235; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
