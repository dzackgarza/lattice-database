The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 71 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{0,2,5,7\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (11 subsets give this class), of order $96$; the smallest realization has degree 196 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 12$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 81, 1174 and 3323; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
