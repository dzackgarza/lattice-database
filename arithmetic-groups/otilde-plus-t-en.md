The 528 orbits of primitive isotropic lines of $\widetilde{O}^+(T_{\mathrm{En}})$ are the 0-cusps of $\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathrm{En}}$, and the 24242 of primitive isotropic planes its 1-cusps; Dutour Sikirić–Hulek, Section 3.6, p. 22, states both numbers.
