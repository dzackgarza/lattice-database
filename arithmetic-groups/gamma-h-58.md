The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 58 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{0,5,7,8\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (30 subsets give this class), of order $480$; the smallest realization has degree 108 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 8$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 53, 512 and 1433; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
