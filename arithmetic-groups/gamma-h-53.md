The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 53 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{1,2,7,8\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (20 subsets give this class), of order $720$; the smallest realization has degree 96 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 8$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 48, 413 and 1159; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
