The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 67 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{1,2,3,7,8\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (30 subsets give this class), of order $144$; the smallest realization has degree 156 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 10$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 80, 1002 and 2884; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
