The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 84 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \{4,3\}$ of the Coxeter diagram (9 subsets give this class), of order $6$; the smallest realization has degree 532 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 19$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 256, 7242 and 21471; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
