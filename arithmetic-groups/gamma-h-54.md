The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 54 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{0,1,5\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (14 subsets give this class), of order $576$; the smallest realization has degree 100 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 8$.

Its orbits of isotropic lines, planes and flags in $N = U \oplus U(2) \oplus E_8(-2)$ number 45, 429 and 1175; Table 2 of the source, p. 14, states them.
