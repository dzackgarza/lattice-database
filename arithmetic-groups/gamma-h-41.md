The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 41 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \overline{\{2,7,8\}}$ of the Coxeter diagram (13 subsets give this class), of order $2880$; the smallest realization has degree 64 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 6$.
