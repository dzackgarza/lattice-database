The group $\overline{\Gamma}_h$ of case 77 of the 87 numerical polarizations of Enriques surfaces, represented by the subset $S = \{4,6,3,7\}$ of the Coxeter diagram (19 subsets give this class), of order $36$; the smallest realization has degree 260 with $\varphi(h_{\min}) = 13$.
