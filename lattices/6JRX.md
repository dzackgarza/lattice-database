The lattice $\Lambda_{2} = U \oplus U \oplus E_{8}(-1) \oplus E_{8}(-1) \oplus \langle -2 \rangle$ of signature $(2, 19)$, the primitive cohomology $L_{1}$ of a polarized K3 surface of degree $2$ in Scattone's notation: rank $21$, determinant $-131072$, even.

Its $O$-row states the count of orbits of primitive isotropic planes, the $1$-cusps of the Baily–Borel compactification, which Scattone's memoir proves. The $0$-cusp count is absent: Theorem 4.0.1, p. 55, states that $D_k/\Gamma_k$ has exactly $\lfloor (N+2)/2 \rfloor$ zero-dimensional boundary components, for $k = \tilde k N^2$ with $\tilde k$ squarefree — one for $k = 1$, since $N = 1$ — and Section 6, p. 94, restates it as the one point $p$ that $D_k/O_-(L_k)$ gains beside $h(k)$ curves. Both are stated for $\Gamma_k = O^*(L_k)$ and for $O_-(L_k)$, and the corpus states the identity of neither with one of the eight keys of `integral.primitive_orbits`, so the count is left off the card rather than attributed to $O$.
