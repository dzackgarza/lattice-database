The lattice $\Lambda_{2} = U \oplus U \oplus E_{8}(-1) \oplus E_{8}(-1) \oplus \langle -2 \rangle$ of signature $(2, 19)$, the primitive cohomology $L_{1}$ of a polarized K3 surface of degree $2$ in Scattone's notation: rank $21$, determinant $-2$, even.

Its rows state the counts of orbits of primitive isotropic planes, the $1$-cusps of the Baily–Borel compactification — under $O$ 4, which Scattone's Proposition 5.4.7 with Corollary 5.4.8(2), p. 78, and Section 6.2 (Degree two), p. 97, prove, and under $O_-(L_1) = O^+$ 4, the $h(1)$ curves of Section 6, p. 94 — and of primitive isotropic lines, the $0$-cusps: one under $O_-(L_1) = O^+$ (Section 6, p. 94) and one under $\Gamma_1 = O^*(L_1) = \widetilde{O}(L_1) \cap O_-(L_1) = \widetilde{O}^+$ (Theorem 4.0.1, p. 55, whose $\lfloor (N+2)/2 \rfloor$ is one for $N = 1$ in degree $2k = 2$). Those two groups are the card's `O+` and `Otilde+` by Scattone's definitions: $O_-(L)$, the isometries of spinor norm 1 (Section 3.6, p. 50), is the index-two subgroup of $O(L)$ preserving the component $D_k$ of the period domain (Section 1, p. 3), and $O^*(L) = O_-(L) \cap \widetilde{O}(L)$ (Section 3.6, p. 51) is $\Gamma_k$ (Section 1, p. 4).

Scattone states the decomposition on p. 3, equation (1.1.1), of Section 1.1: $L_k = \langle -2k \rangle \oplus H^2 \oplus E_8^2$, the two unimodular $E_8$ summands introduced on p. 2. In this card's basis both $E_8$ summands are negative definite, so each is $-1$ times the Cartan matrix of $E_8$; that matrix is unimodular, and the determinant of the whole sum is therefore $-2k$, with discriminant group $\mathbb{Z}/2k$.
