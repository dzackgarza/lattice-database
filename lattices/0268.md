$L$ is [anisotropic](../theory/indefinite-lattices.html#isotropic): $b(x, x) = 0$ means $x_1^2 + x_2^2 = 3x_3^2$.
The squares modulo 3 are 0 and 1, so $3 \mid x_1$ and $3 \mid x_2$; then $3 \mid x_3$, and descent gives $x = 0$.
It is an odd lattice of signature $(1, 2)$.
