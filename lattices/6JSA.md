The even hyperbolic 2-elementary lattice of invariants $(r, a, \delta) = (9, 7, 1)$: rank $9$, signature $(1, 8)$, discriminant group $(\mathbb{Z}/2)^{7}$, $\delta = 1$.

The Gram matrix is the orthogonal sum $U(2) \oplus \langle -2 \rangle \oplus \langle -2 \rangle \oplus \langle -2 \rangle \oplus D_{4}(-1)$ in the displayed basis. Nikulin's Theorem 3.6.2, p. 144, states that for $t_+, t_- > 0$ these invariants determine the isomorphism class, so this presentation is that class.
