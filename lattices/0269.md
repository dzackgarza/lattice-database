$L$ is [anisotropic](../theory/indefinite-lattices.html#isotropic): $b(x, x) = 0$ means $x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 = 7x_4^2$.
For $x_4$ odd the right side is $7$ modulo $8$, which is not a sum of three squares modulo 8. For $x_4$ even the left side is $0$ modulo $4$, so $x_1$, $x_2$, $x_3$ are even, and descent gives $x = 0$.
It is an odd lattice of signature $(1, 3)$.
