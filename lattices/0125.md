This is the even hyperbolic 2-elementary lattice with [invariants](../theory/discriminant-forms.html#two-elementary) $(r, a, \delta) = (10, 10, 0)$.

Since $a = r$, $L^* = \tfrac{1}{2} L$, and $L$ is the twist $M(2)$ of the unimodular lattice $M = U \oplus E_{8}(-1)$, the record [0128](../tag/0128.html).
The corpus records a twist $M(n)$, $|n| \geq 2$, only for the 13 rows $a = r$ of Nikulin's table, because the classification names the twist and not $M$.

Nikulin lists it in Table 1 of *Factor groups of groups of automorphisms of hyperbolic forms with respect to subgroups generated by 2-reflections* (1983), the table of the even hyperbolic lattices $L$ with $2 A_L = 0$ that have a primitive embedding in the K3 lattice.
These are the invariant lattices of the nonsymplectic involutions of K3 surfaces (Remark 4.5.3), and Theorem 4.3.2 states that $(r, a, \delta)$ determines each of them.

$U$ is the lattice with basis $e, f$ and $b(e, e) = b(f, f) = 0$, $b(e, f) = 1$.
$L(k)$ is the module $L$ with the form $k b$.
$E_8$ is the root lattice of that type: $e_1, \dots, e_8$ is a basis of simple roots, numbered as SageMath's `CartanMatrix` numbers them, and $b(e_i, e_j)$ is the entry $(i, j)$ of the Cartan matrix.
The basis of an orthogonal sum is the union of the bases of the summands, in the order written.
