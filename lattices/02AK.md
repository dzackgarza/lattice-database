Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 77 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{77} \cong T_{192}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{37} \cong T_{24}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{69} \cong (Q_8 * Q_8) \rtimes C_3$ of Table 10.2, and this record is the lattice $M$ with $\Lambda^G = M(4)$; the corpus records $M$ and not its twist.
