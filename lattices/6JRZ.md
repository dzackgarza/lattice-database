The even hyperbolic 2-elementary lattice of invariants $(r, a, \delta) = (4, 4, 1)$: rank $4$, signature $(1, 3)$, discriminant group $(\mathbb{Z}/2)^{4}$, $\delta = 1$.

The Gram matrix is the orthogonal sum $U(2) \oplus \langle -2 \rangle \oplus \langle -2 \rangle$ in the displayed basis; its discriminant group has 4 generators of order $2$.
Nikulin, Theorem 3.6.2, p. 144, states that for $t_+, t_- > 0$ the invariants $(\delta; t_+, t_-, a)$ determine the isomorphism class of an even 2-elementary lattice, so this presentation is that class.
