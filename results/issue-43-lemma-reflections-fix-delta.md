# #43, one lemma: every Coble chamber reflection fixes $\delta$, and it therefore cannot move a class

**Lemma.** At each of Sterk's cusps `(4.3.7)`, `(4.3.8)`, `(4.3.9)`, the reflection in any wall of the chamber that lies
in $T_{\mathrm{Co}} = \delta^\perp$ is an element of $\Gamma_{\mathrm{Co},2}$ fixing the cusp, and no such reflection
carries one root class of $K_I$ to another. Consequently the reflection subgroup $W_v$ is inside the $\Gamma_{\mathrm{Co},2}$-cusp
stabilizer and is invisible on the root classes: whatever the count at `(4.3.9)` is, $W_v$ does not decide it.

**Proof.** For a wall $w \in T_{\mathrm{Co}}$ the reflection is $s_w(x) = x - 2\,b(x,w)/b(w,w)\,w$. Since
$b(\delta, w) = 0$ by $w \in \delta^\perp$,

$$s_w(\delta) = \delta - \frac{2\,b(\delta,w)}{b(w,w)}\,w = \delta,$$

so $s_w$ preserves $T_{\mathrm{Co}}$, and being a reflection it lies in $\Gamma_{\mathrm{En}}$ with $s_w(v) = v$ at the
cusp. Its class action: the setwise stabilizer of a wall's mirror in a Coxeter group is the parabolic generated by that
wall and the walls orthogonal to it, so two walls in one orbit have equal orthogonal neighbour sets; the root walls at
each $\Gamma_{\mathrm{Co},2}$-cusp have pairwise *different* neighbour sets — computed from card 029J's Gram tensor and asserted for the
$T_{En}$ chambers at `tests/test_cusp_root_orbits.py:153` — so $W_v$ fixes each class. The walls in $T_{\mathrm{Co}}$
number 9, 9 and 11 at the three cusps, out of 12, 11 and 14.

**What it settles and what it does not.** It settles that the constructive part of the $\Gamma_{\mathrm{Co},2}$-cusp stabilizer that the
card's chamber supplies — its reflection subgroup — is inside $\Gamma_{\mathrm{Co},2}$ and moves no root class, which is
what the walk over the owner's family measured (`results/probe-43-coble-classes.py`: 22 of 27 members preserve
$T_{\mathrm{Co}}$, none merges). It does not settle the count: the identification of $\alpha_{13}$ with $\alpha_{14}$
comes from the diagram symmetry that also fixes $\delta$
(`results/issue-43-lemma-alpha13-alpha14.md`), and whether $\alpha_{12}$ joins them needs an element that is neither a
reflection nor a symmetry of the chamber.

**Sources.** H. Sterk, *Compactifications of the period space of Enriques surfaces, Part I*, Math. Z. 207 (1991), 1–36:
`(4.3.7)`, p. 22, `(4.3.8)`, p. 23 and `(4.3.9)`, p. 24 print the chambers whose walls are used; `(4.3.10)`, p. 25,
identifies their span as $\mathbb{Z}\{e-f, e+f\} \oplus E_8(-2)$, which contains $\delta = e - f$ — the fact that makes
$b(\delta, w)$ the right pairing to test. The wall counts and pairings are card 029J's, not the paper's.
