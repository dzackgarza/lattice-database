## The operation that is missing

A selection over `rho_L^{-1}(P)`'s Schreier family that does not first materialise it — for example
`discriminant_point_stabilizer_image_generators(point, family=...)`, or a variant whose loop reads
`family.value(label)` from a streaming transversal. With it, the greedy rule the docstring states
(add a member when its image is not yet in the subgroup, stop when the order reaches `|P|`) returns
the nine members at the cost of the members examined, not of the whole family.

No existing public operation substitutes for it. The stabilizers this module does offer —
`pointwise_stabilizer` (`:4409`), `setwise_stabilizer` (`:4451`), `stabilizer` (`:4279`),
`vector_stabilizer_generators` (`:4812`), `isotropic_stabilizer_generators` (`:5509`) — stabilise
lattice data, not a point of `A_L`; `:3949` and `:3995` are the only pair over `A_L`'s points.
